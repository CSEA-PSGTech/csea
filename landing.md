### Important Visual Behavior

These should NOT look like standard UI labels.

They should feel like someone has annotated a physical editorial photo wall with a blue pen.

Use:
- handwritten / marker-like typography
- slightly imperfect letter spacing
- small uppercase text
- thin hand-drawn arrows
- slightly curved arrows rather than perfectly straight SVG arrows
- subtle rotation, approximately -3° to +3°
- different annotation positions for each photo

Do NOT make every annotation identical.

Do NOT put the text inside the photos.

Do NOT use speech bubbles.

Do NOT use boxed labels.

Do NOT use thick cartoon arrows.

The annotations should remain subtle and secondary to the photos.

### Animation

On hero entrance:
- photos gently fade/slide into position
- annotations appear shortly after their corresponding photo
- arrow draws itself from start to end
- text fades/slides in very slightly

Keep the animation premium and restrained.

On hover:
- photo can have a very subtle movement
- annotation should remain stable or move slightly with the photo

Do not make annotations continuously bounce or animate.

### Desktop Layout

Use the existing 3-column grid.

The central area must remain relatively clean for:

"we are CSEA ;)"

and the CSEA graphic.

Place annotations in the negative space surrounding the photo cards.

Make sure annotations never overlap the main hero typography.

### Responsive Behavior

Desktop:
- show most annotations
- use curved arrows
- allow annotations to occupy negative space around photos

Tablet:
- reduce annotation count
- reduce arrow size

Mobile:
- hide most annotations
- keep only 2–3 important annotations
- never allow annotations to cause horizontal overflow
- reposition them relative to their photo
